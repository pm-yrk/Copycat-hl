# Compliance and positioning notes

This product should be positioned as:

```text
market intelligence
wallet-flow analytics
research dashboard
```

Avoid marketing language that implies guaranteed profits or personalised recommendations.

Use warnings such as:

```text
This dashboard is for informational and research purposes only. It is not financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any asset. Cryptoassets are high risk and can result in loss.
```

Before selling publicly, speak to a solicitor/compliance adviser familiar with crypto promotions in your target jurisdictions.

## Data licensing

If using LegacyExternalProvider for candidate discovery, check your API license before selling access to products derived from the data. If needed, replace LegacyExternalProvider with your own indexer/discovery layer.
